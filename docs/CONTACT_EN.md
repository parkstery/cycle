## 2. How to Contact

- **In the App**  
  Use the **Contact** item in the app menu or the channels shown on the settings screen.

- **Store or official page**  
  Use the contact information on the App’s store page (web or mobile) or on **LiveOnSoft**’s official project or website.

- **Project repository**  
  For open source, technical questions, bug reports, or feature suggestions, use the repository’s issue or contact channel (e.g. GitHub) when available.

Specific email, form, or URL may vary by app version and distribution; **please refer to the latest information in the App or on the store/official page** for current contact details.
