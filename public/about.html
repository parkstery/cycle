Ride the World – Indoor Cycling

Plan routes on real maps, review elevation, and simulate rides indoors. Where Mapillary coverage exists, optional street-level imagery can play along the route together with slope-aware coaching.

Features

  • Virtual riding on routes worldwide (subject to app restrictions)
  • Gradient-aware coaching and elevation profiles
  • Optional Mapillary street-level imagery along the route
  • Interactive Mapbox map (standard and satellite-style layers)
  • Optional BLE sensors or smart trainers (read-only); preferences and calibration stay on your device

Technology Stack

Frontend
React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
Maps & Data
Mapbox GL JS; OSRM routing; Mapbox Geocoding and/or Nominatim (OpenStreetMap); Open-Elevation / OpenTopoData (and optional Valhalla-based height); Mapillary imagery API when used.
