Privacy Policy

Ride the World – Indoor Cycling
Last updated: March 2026

1. Overview

Ride the World – Indoor Cycling (the “App”) provides indoor cycling route simulation and map and route exploration services. This Privacy Policy describes the information the App collects, uses, and stores, and how third-party services are used.

Developer/Operator: LiveOnSoft
App type: Client (browser/web app) based; the App does not store personal information on its own servers.

2. Information Collected and Used

2.1 Information You Enter

  • Route search input: Start, end, and waypoint addresses or place names
    Purpose: Route search, map display, elevation and distance calculation.
    This information is included in “Stored data” only when you choose to save it.
  • Place search terms: Place names or addresses entered in the search bar.
    Purpose: Display search results.
    Search terms may be stored in a recent search list (optional, on-device only).

2.2 Information Used Automatically from Your Device or Browser

  • Location (optional): The App may read your current location once, with your consent, via the browser’s Geolocation API.
    Purpose: Set the initial map center.
    This information is not sent to our servers and is not used if permission is denied.
  • Technical information: Browser type, screen size, connection environment, etc., may be transmitted when the App communicates with third-party services (map, route, and elevation APIs).
    Purpose: Service functionality and compatibility.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, phone number, account information, or payment details.
  • The App does not require login or account registration.

3. Stored Data (On Device)

The App does not store user data on its own servers. The following information is stored only in your device’s browser storage (localStorage).

Stored item Content Purpose
favorite_routes Saved routes (start, end, waypoints, route geometry, etc.) Restore My Routes
recent_places Recent place search terms Search convenience
  • Storage and deletion are controlled by user actions (save or delete buttons).
  • Clearing the App’s data or browser storage will remove this information.

4. Third-Party Services and Information Shared

The App uses external services for route search, map display, elevation lookup, and Street View features. Data handling is governed by each provider’s policy.

Service Information that may be sent Purpose Policy
OpenStreetMap / Nominatim Search terms, coordinates (reverse geocoding) Address/place search, coordinate conversion OSM Foundation Policies
OSRM Start, end, waypoint coordinates Route calculation Policy of the deployment server
Open-Elevation (or alternative elevation API) Coordinates along the route Elevation data Policy of the API provider
Google Maps / Street View Coordinates for map display and Street View requests Map rendering and imagery Google Privacy Policy

These services may process IP address, request time, coordinates, and search terms under their own privacy policies. The App does not sell or share data with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Retention

  • On-device stored data: Remains on your device until you delete it or clear browser storage.
  • Third-party services: Retention is governed by each provider’s policy.

6. Your Rights

  • You may delete saved routes through the My Routes feature.
  • You may remove recent searches using in-app options or by clearing browser storage.
  • Location permission may be denied or revoked through browser or device settings.
  • For questions or requests related to personal information, please contact the developer using the information provided on the app store or official project page.

7. Security

  • Use of the App over HTTPS or other encrypted connections is recommended.
  • User data is not stored on our servers.
  • Third-party API services apply their own security and privacy policies.

8. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If a parent or guardian believes a child’s information has been collected, please contact us.

9. Policy Changes

  • This Privacy Policy may be updated due to legal or service changes.
  • Major changes may be announced through the App, version notes, or official channels.
  • Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Applicable Law and Jurisdiction

For users in the Republic of Korea, the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws may apply. Disputes related to personal information processing will be handled by courts with jurisdiction under applicable law.
